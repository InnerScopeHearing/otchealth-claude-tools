# Context continuity

Create a small continuation capsule pointing to source-owned evidence. A capsule is no proof of destination access.

Capture objective, accepted scope, source owner, actual identity/client/host evidence, original intent, verified state, next action and dependencies. Carry original start/deadline, cumulative attempts, usage/spend limits and one writer per target. Context loss never resets these.

Record unresolved writes with provider/job/worker/artifact handles, supported idempotency key, exact target/pre-state, last status and next reconciliation read. Distinguish acknowledged live jobs from unknown submissions. Freeze conflicting writes until unknown results are reconciled.

For indirect AI OS work preserve role, client, job_id, worker_id, artifact_id, source identifiers, offsets, expected SHA256 and status when present. Use actual exposed handoff/contract retrieval, artifact fetch and verification tools. Report missing methods instead of guessing names. Download alone does not establish integrity, currentness or completion.

On resume retrieve only evidence needed for the next decision and verify destination runtime and own-seat bindings. Preserve originals until continuity and restore are accepted. New successor conversations require their own authorization and identity proof.

Retain built/tested/reviewed/merged/deployed/accepted separately. Resolve contradictions using newer authoritative evidence; preserve the superseded record and reason. Reuse IDs/hashes to avoid broad repeated retrieval.

Exclude credential values, PHI, privileged personal legal bodies and MNPI. Protected seats retain their own identity/storage/restore gates.

Return capsule with verified state, exact pointers, unknowns, remaining deadlines/budget and one next action. The receiver checks its own access.
