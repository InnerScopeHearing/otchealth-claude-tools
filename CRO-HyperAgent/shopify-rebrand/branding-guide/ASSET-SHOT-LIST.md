# iHEAR TReO — Full Visual Asset Shot List
### Source: Shopify Living Doc Section 10 (CRO research, 2026-07-10). This is what Folio should actually produce. Total target: ~35-45 photographs + 4-5 videos — enough that no section of the site/email/advertorial system repeats the same handful of images.

**Governing principle across every asset below:** real customers (diverse ages 55-80+, skewing toward "still working / still social," not frail) and real credentialed staff (Mark/Kim), shot in natural/warm light, in real environments. Reject "product on white" clinical shots as a primary treatment (acceptable only as one supporting angle, never the hero). No stock people. No AI-generated humans anywhere — this is a hard compliance/trust rule, not a style preference (see COMPLIANCE-AND-APPROVED-COPY.md).

## (a) Homepage Hero — 4-6 assets
- 1 short looping product-in-use video (6-10 sec, works muted, captioned): a real customer at a dinner table, mid-laugh, catching a joke they'd have missed. Visualizes the approved hook "the three words you're tired of saying at dinner." Warm restaurant/home-dinner lighting (~2700-3200K), medium shot, device visible but not the focal point — the relief/connection is the focal point.
- 2-3 static hero fallback/variant stills for A/B testing and mobile crop: same subject/scene family, different micro-moments (leaning in to hear a grandchild, laughing at a group table, walking with a friend mid-conversation).
- Emotional beat: relief + reconnection, never disability/deficit. Never a person cupping their ear or looking frustrated in hero imagery — start at the resolved moment.

## (b) Product Page Gallery — 8-10 images + 1-2 videos per SKU (single vs. pair)
Sequence matters (based on the Lexie Hearing pattern, adapted warmer/less clinical):
1. Lifestyle hero shot (in-use, warm environment).
2. Looping in-use video (device going in/out of ear, or a moment of hearing something — a bird, a doorbell, a whisper).
3. Macro/detail shot of the device on a warm neutral surface (linen, wood grain), soft directional light — craftsmanship, not sterility.
4. Size-comparison shot — device next to a coin, fingertip, or earbud case for scale.
5. In-ear demonstration shot — real ear, real (consented, paid) customer or staff. Highest-trust, most-often-skipped shot in the category. Do not fake with a stock ear.
6. Pair/kit contents flat-lay — device(s), charging case, cleaning tool, manual — styled, not a sterile accessory grid.
7. Packaging/unboxing moment — reinforce the "doesn't feel like a $99 product" premium-unboxing beat.
8. Second lifestyle shot in a different context (outdoors, workplace, family gathering).
- Aim for 30-40% unique assets between the single and pair SKU listings.

## (c) Trust/Credibility Section — 8-10 assets
- Founder/clinician portraits: Mark and Kim, same backdrop family/lighting rig/wardrobe register (approachable-professional, not lab coat, not casual) — must read as a matched set.
- 1 clinician-in-context shot: Mark or Kim actually demonstrating/fitting a device with a customer (doubles as credibility + "how it works" support image).
- 4-6 real customer portraits, varied ages/settings, each in an environment relevant to their own story (a workshop, a porch, a kitchen table) — NOT all shot against one studio backdrop.
- Emotional beat: competence + warmth (different beat than the hero's "I want this feeling").

## (d) Comparison/Pricing Section — 1-2 supporting images (table graphic itself is a design asset, see BRAND-STRATEGY-BRIEF.md)
- 1 supporting lifestyle image beside/behind the pricing module — not another product-on-white shot; reinforce that cheaper ≠ lesser experience.
- Optional: 1 "what's included" flat-lay if bundling accessories/warranty into the price story.

## (e) FAQ/Education Section
- 1 labeled static cutaway diagram (device/ear) — see BRAND-STRATEGY-BRIEF.md for the compliance-safe format.
- 1 short (45-90 sec) narrated video walking through fitting/adjustment (Mark or Kim) — doubles as onboarding/post-purchase content.
- 3-5 simple line-icon illustration pairs for common FAQ answers (battery life, water resistance, adjustability, trial period, compatibility).

## (f) Email Creative (Reactivation Send) — 0 new shoots, re-crop existing assets
Echo the site, don't duplicate it: reuse hero video stills + the customer-portrait set, recomposed for email's narrower single-column format (must read at ~300px thumbnail width). 1 hero image/GIF (same dinner-table moment as site hero, for recognition), 1 product-in-hand/in-ear proof shot, 1 customer-testimonial pairing (portrait + quote) reused from (c).

## (g) Advertorial/Quiz Funnel Page — 3-4 unique + reuse
Photography register shifts toward documentary/"real person telling their story," slightly less polished than brand-hero content:
- 1-2 "problem moment" images — the ONE place in the whole library where showing the *before* (leaning in at a table, a missed joke's beat of confusion) is appropriate; not allowed anywhere else.
- 1 quiz-result/persona-matching graphic (illustrated, not photographic).
- 1-2 resolution/after images pulled from the customer-portrait set in (c).
